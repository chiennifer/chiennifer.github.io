excerpt: 'AI systems are increasingly tasked to complete responsibilities with decreasing oversight. This delegation requires users to accept certain risks, typically mitigated by perceived or actual alignment of values between humans and AI. Stochastic behavior threatens to undermine this alignment. We take a philosophical perspective on the tension between stochasticity and trustworthiness, introduce a novel definition of stochasticity, and propose latent value modeling for both AI systems and users to better assess alignment.'
