excerpt: 'Toxicity scoring may render pertinent information inaccessible, rigidify cultural norms, and prevent language reclamation processes, particularly for marginalized people. We extend algorithmic recourse to generative language models, giving users a mechanism to achieve desired predictions by dynamically setting toxicity-filtering thresholds. A pilot study (n=30) supports improvements in usability over fixed-threshold filtering.'
