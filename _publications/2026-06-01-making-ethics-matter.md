excerpt: 'Computer science (CS) increasingly shapes our lives, making ethical reasoning imperative for CS students. To address this demand, some CS departments have adopted Embedded Ethics, a pedagogical approach that weaves ethics into technical content. We theorize that, though aiming to connect ethical dilemmas to students' experiences, Embedded Ethics curricula can end up singling out minority populations as being harmed by technology. Drawing from Embedded Ethics coursework examples, we articulate how this pedagogical approach may alienate both minority and non-minority CS students. We make calls for more broadly relatable framing of ethical dilemmas to engage all CS students.'
